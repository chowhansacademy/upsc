Daily Skill Test

UPSC GS-I Challenge of the Day

Score: 0 / 3

1. With reference to the Executive Power of the President of India, consider the following statements:

Explanation: Under Article 77, all executive actions are taken in the President's name and the President makes rules for transaction of government business.

2. Which of the following is/are part of the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of RBI?

Explanation: The MPC consists of 6 members: RBI Governor (Chairperson), Deputy Governor, one RBI officer, and 3 members appointed by the Central Government.

3. The 'Dandi March' organized by Mahatma Gandhi was against which colonial law?

Explanation: The Salt March (March-April 1930) was a direct action campaign of tax resistance against the British salt monopoly.
